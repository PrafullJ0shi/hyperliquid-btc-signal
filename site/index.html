      Planned fields: score in [−1, 1], posts scored, bullish and bearish shares, look-back window, method.

      Method. Each wallet with a BTC position votes long or short. The vote is weighted by log(1 + one-month perp PnL), shrunk for equity below $100,000, and scaled by BTC notional over equity (capped at 3). P(long) is the share of that weight on the long side. The interval is a 95% bootstrap over traders. Verdict is LONG at or above 0.60, SHORT at or below 0.40, otherwise NEUTRAL. Flats do not vote.

      The machine-readable snapshot is data/signal.json. The method, filters, and limitations are in the repository README.

      This is not financial advice and not a forecast. It is a descriptive reading of public positions at the issue time. The figure can be wrong. Wallets can be market makers, copy one another, or change between polls. Do not trade on this page.
